BOTH eyes, nose and mouth belong to a complete natural human face, fully uncovered. Absolutely no half-doll face, no mask over the face, no wooden or mechanical face, no face stitches. Human ears. A relaxed ready-to-walk standing pose viewed three-quarter toward screen RIGHT, feet both oriented toward screen right with a small natural fore-aft separation. Clear silhouette, weight supported naturally. Entire figure, tools, frame and dangling doll comfortably within frame with transparent margins.

LEGENDARY EQUIPMENT: A luxurious dark petrol-teal fitted long coat/waistcoat with short split tails and subtle embroidered puppet motifs, burgundy scarf, layered charcoal fabric, leather straps and antique warm gold/brass ornamental armor at shoulders, forearms and boots. Both weapons fitted to human arms and aesthetically coordinated with coat and frame. The scissors and string apparatus are signature equipment, not guns or bulky robot arms.

No photorealism, no glossy 3D rendering, no smooth airbrushed illustration, no excessive tiny jewelry. Keep foreground limbs clearly separated from torso and cape.

DELIVERY: Only one complete isolated character on a truly transparent alpha background. No colored background, no magenta, no checkerboard painted into image, no ground plane, no cast ground shadow, no labels, no title, no text, no watermark, no comparison views, no decorative border.
